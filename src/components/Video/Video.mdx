## Overview

The VideoPlayer component provides a fully-featured, accessible video player with custom controls, built-in support for common video features, and seamless integration with your design system. It includes play/pause controls, volume management, progress tracking, fullscreen support, and comprehensive keyboard navigation.

## Accessibility

Our VideoPlayer component follows accessibility best practices:

- **Keyboard Navigation**: Full support for Tab, Enter, and Space key interactions
- **Screen Reader Support**: Proper ARIA labels and semantic HTML structure
- **Focus Management**: Clear focus indicators and logical tab order
- **Color Contrast**: High contrast controls that meet WCAG guidelines
- **Reduced Motion**: Respects user's motion preferences
- **Captions Support**: Built-in support for WebVTT caption files

### Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Space** or **K**: Play/pause video
- **Enter**: Play/pause video (when focused on play button)
- **Tab/Shift+Tab**: Navigate between controls
- **Arrow Keys**: Seek through video (when progress bar is focused)
- **M**: Toggle mute
- **F**: Toggle fullscreen
- **J** or **Arrow Left**: Seek backward 10 seconds
- **L** or **Arrow Right**: Seek forward 10 seconds
- **Arrow Up**: Increase volume
- **Arrow Down**: Decrease volume

## Best Practices

### Do's ✅

- Always provide a meaningful `poster` image for better user experience
- Use appropriate `preload` settings based on your use case
- Consider video file size and format for optimal loading
- Test videos across different devices and network conditions
- Provide captions for accessibility when content requires them
- Use the `muted` prop for autoplay videos to ensure compatibility

### Don'ts ❌

- Don't rely on autoplay without user consent (use muted autoplay)
- Avoid very large video files that could cause performance issues
- Don't hide controls completely unless there's a specific UX reason
- Avoid nesting video players or placing them in small containers
- Don't use videos for content that could be conveyed with text or images

### Performance Optimization

```jsx
// Recommended: Optimized loading
<VideoPlayer
  src="/videos/optimized-video.mp4"
  poster="/images/video-poster.jpg"
  preload="metadata"
  className="aspect-video"
/>

// For hero videos: Consider lazy loading
<VideoPlayer
  src="/videos/hero-video.mp4"
  poster="/images/hero-poster.jpg"
  preload="none"
  className="aspect-video"
/>
```

### Responsive Design

```jsx
// Responsive video container
<div className="aspect-video w-full">
    <VideoPlayer
        src="/videos/responsive-video.mp4"
        poster="/images/video-poster.jpg"
        controls={true}
        showCustomControls={true}
    />
</div>
```

## Browser Support

The VideoPlayer component supports all modern browsers that implement the HTML5 video element:

- **Chrome**: Full support (recommended)
- **Firefox**: Full support
- **Safari**: Full support
- **Edge**: Full support
- **Mobile browsers**: Full support with touch controls

### Video Format Support

The component supports the following video formats:

- **MP4** (H.264): Universally supported
- **WebM** (VP8/VP9): Modern browser support
- **OGV** (Theora): Limited support
